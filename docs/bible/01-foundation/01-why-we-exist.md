# Why We Exist

## The problem

Plans often fail between online intention and physical attendance because details are unclear, joining is awkward, trust is weak, or coordination is scattered across chats.

## The answer

GO IRL turns a vague plan into a clear local activity:

```text
create -> share -> join/request -> coordinate -> meet
```

Mission: **Less scrolling. More life.**

## User promise

A participant should quickly understand what, when, where, who, capacity, join state, and what happens next.

An organizer should create and share a small activity without repeatedly explaining the same details.

## Why Telegram and Olomouc

Telegram is where informal coordination already happens. Olomouc provides a focused market where event density and repeat participation can be tested without an empty multi-city catalog.

## Current phase

Closed Beta completed on 2026-07-20. The next task is not to invent a larger product; it is to prepare the proven loop for release without overstating public readiness.
