# Bible Product Philosophy

## Core belief

People do not need another feed. They need a short, trustworthy bridge from intention to real-life action.

Mission: **Less scrolling. More life.**

## Product constraints

- Optimize for real attendance, not time in app.
- Prefer small local activities before broad catalogs.
- Build trust before scale.
- Use Telegram as the first coordination surface, not as a reason to build a new social network.
- Keep Browser Demo Mode local-only.
- Reject addictive loops, vanity metrics, and passive consumption.
- Treat privacy and safety as product requirements.

## Success definition

```text
I saw it, understood it, joined it, coordinated briefly, and actually went.
```

The primary product outcome is Join-to-show-up conversion, not screen time.

## Scope rule

Closed Beta proved the Olomouc baseline. Release Preparation protects that loop. Long-term verticals, AI discovery, reputation, ticketing, payments, dating, and broad multi-city expansion remain future unless explicitly approved and implemented.
