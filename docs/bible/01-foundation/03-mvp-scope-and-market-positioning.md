# Book I — MVP Scope and Market Positioning

## One-line definition

GO IRL is a Telegram-first local meetup layer for people who want to spend less time scrolling and more time participating in real life.

Mission: **Less scrolling. More life.**

## Current lifecycle

Closed Beta completed on 2026-07-20. The current phase is Release Preparation and focused post-beta stabilization. Broad public launch is not claimed.

## Proven baseline

Olomouc, Czechia, and the six canonical beta categories:

1. Volleyball
2. Running
3. Walking
4. Coffee meetup
5. Board games
6. Language exchange

These categories are historical acceptance evidence and the default release baseline, not permission for automatic expansion.

## Current product promise

Create a small local activity quickly, share it through Telegram, make join state clear, coordinate in Activity Chat, and meet in real life.

## Not GO IRL

- social network or feed;
- ticketing or payment platform;
- club CRM;
- dating product;
- permanent messenger;
- generic city event calendar;
- AI content stream.

## Expansion rule

Any new category, city, vertical, marketplace, or high-risk social feature requires a reviewed product decision, runtime design, safety review, and updated source-of-truth documentation.
