# Core Principles

1. **Real life over screen time.**
2. **The create-share-join-chat-attend loop is sacred.**
3. **Telegram-first, not Telegram-only forever.**
4. **Local density beats empty scale.**
5. **Small events first.**
6. **Beginner comfort is a product requirement.**
7. **Trust is part of the flow, not only a badge.**
8. **Coach is sport-specific; other roles require their own names and approval.**
9. **Demo identity is never production identity.**
10. **Trusted auth, RLS, and backend authority cannot be weakened by documentation.**
11. **Stability before expansion.**
12. **Competitors are signals, not instructions.**
13. **Privacy and safety before high-risk growth.**
14. **Documentation must separate current truth from future vision.**
15. **The smallest reliable product wins.**

Mission: **Less scrolling. More life.**

The proven Closed-Beta baseline remains Olomouc and the six canonical categories. Release Preparation may improve reliability and operations, but it does not automatically authorize new categories, verticals, or public-launch claims.
