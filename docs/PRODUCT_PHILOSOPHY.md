# GO IRL Product Philosophy

## Mission

**Less scrolling. More life.**

GO IRL exists to move people from passive scrolling and fragmented chats into real participation.

## What we build

A platform for small local offline activities. The best session is short: understand, join, coordinate, close the app, and live.

## What we do not build

GO IRL is not a social network, endless feed, popularity contest, generic event calendar, permanent messenger, or attention business.

## Core entity

`Activity` is the current product foundation. Future vertical experiences may specialize the flow, but they must share the same real-life purpose and must not be described as shipped before implementation and approval.

## Trust, privacy, and safety

Collect only necessary data. Production identity and permissions must be backend/RLS-enforced. High-risk features require moderation, reporting, consent, and abuse protection before launch.

## Technology rule

AI and automation may shorten the path to a real meeting. They must not become the product goal, replace human judgment, or create an addictive content loop.

## Current lifecycle

Closed Beta completed on 2026-07-20. Release Preparation now protects the proven Olomouc loop. The six beta categories remain the default release baseline; expansion requires a reviewed product decision.
